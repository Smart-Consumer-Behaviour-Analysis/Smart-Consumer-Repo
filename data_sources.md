## Collection notes
For each source, document:
1. Why it is relevant to the project.
2. How the team obtained the data.
3. The period and population represented.
4. Any sampling, missing-data, or quality limitations.
5. Whether redistribution in this public repository is permitted.

## Responsible data handling
Do not publish personal, confidential, or sensitive consumer information. If the dataset cannot legally or safely be shared, provide source instructions and a non-sensitive sample schema instead.
